03
METHODOLOGY
Method Overview
The buffer waits for the missing predecessor




  Bind every snapshot to an “asset_id” and a producer version before comparing it.
  Deduplicate by record identity before emission.
  Sort retained records by producer version, not by arrival time.
  Emit only when two records belong to the same asset and the successor version is
  exactly one greater.
  Keep unresolved gaps, conflicts, late records, and evictions as diagnostics.
